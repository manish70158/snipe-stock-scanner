## Edge 1: HV1 (Highest Volume Day 1)

*The Institutional Entry Signal*

### Definition
The stock prints its HIGHEST VOLUME in 50+ days on a single day with price closing in the upper 60% of the day's range.

### What It Means
- Big money (MFs, FIIs) initiated a position
- They CANNOT exit in one day — they'll support the stock
- Follow the smart money footprint

### Chartink Detection Code
```
latest volume > latest max(50, volume) and
latest close > latest low + 0.6 * (latest high - latest low)
```

### Key Rule
HV1 is valid for 10 trading days. If no base forms within 10 days, ignore.

### India Example
CG Power, Jul 2024 — Volume 3.9× 50DMA on pivot breakout → +80% in 3 months

---

## Edge 2: HVE (High Volume Earnings)

*Post-Result Momentum*

### Definition
Stock gaps up or surges 5%+ on earnings day with volume ≥ 2× 50DMA volume.

### What It Means
- Market is repricing the stock higher after new fundamental information
- Institutions are accumulating on results
- Creates a new "floor" — stock rarely returns below earnings gap

### How to Detect
- Track NSE quarterly results calendar
- Day after results: check if volume > 2× average AND price gap > 5%
- Mark the gap zone — this becomes support

### Key Rule
Wait 3-5 days after HVE for a pullback to the gap zone. Don't chase the spike.

### India Example
Waaree Energies, Q2FY25 — EPS +45%, stock +12% on 4.2× volume → formed base → +60% from pullback entry

---

## Edge 3: RS Edge (Relative Strength)

*Outperforming During Market Correction*

### Definition
While Nifty 500 corrects 5%+, the stock corrects less than 50% of Nifty's decline (or stays flat/rises).

### What It Means
- Institutions are holding through the correction (they don't sell)
- When market recovers, this stock will lead
- Relative strength = institutional conviction

### How to Calculate
```
RS Ratio = Stock % Change (21-day) / Nifty 500 % Change (21-day)
RS Edge = TRUE if RS Ratio > 2.0 during Nifty decline of >5%
```

### Chartink Condition
```
latest close / latest offset(21, close) >
1.5 * (latest nifty 500 close / latest offset(21, nifty 500 close))
```

### India Example
Cochin Shipyard, May 2024 — Nifty -4.8%, Cochin Shipyard +2.1% → RS Ratio: extreme → Stock +120% next 4 months

## Position Sizing Formula

### The 1% Rule (Never Risk More Than 1% Per Trade)

```
Risk Amount = Account Size × 1%
Share Quantity = Risk Amount ÷ (Entry Price - Stop Price)
Position Value = Share Quantity × Entry Price
```

### Example

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Account Size | ₹10,00,000 |
| Risk % | 1% |
| Risk Amount | ₹10,000 |
| Entry Price | ₹412 |
| Stop-Loss | ₹378 |
| Risk per Share | ₹34 |
| Shares to Buy | 294 |
| Position Value | ₹1,21,128 (12% of account) |

**Key Insight:** Position size is determined by your STOP distance, not by how much you "like" the stock.

### How to Enter (The Right Way)

| Rule | Details |
|------|---------|
| **Order Type** | LIMIT order only. Never market orders. |
| **Entry Trigger** | Price breaks above pivot on volume ≥ 1.5× 50DMA |

### The Pivot Point
- VCP: Top of the last contraction
- Flat Base: Resistance level of the range
- Cup & Handle: Top of the handle

## Stop-Loss Rules

### Non-Negotiable Exit Points

| Stop Type | Placement | When to Use |
|-----------|-----------|-------------|
| **Initial Stop** | Below base low or VCP low | At entry (always set) |
| **Breakeven Stop** | Move to entry price | After stock gains +10-15% |
| **Trailing Stop** | Below 10-DMA or 21-EMA | After stock gains +20%+ |
| **Time Stop** | Exit if no progress in 3 weeks | Stock dead after entry |

### Stop-Loss Placement Rules

1. Stop MUST be below a logical price level (not arbitrary %)
2. Maximum stop distance: 8% from entry
3. If stop requires >8%, the entry is too late — skip the trade
4. NEVER widen a stop after entry
5. NEVER remove a stop to "give it room"

## VCP: Volatility Contraction Pattern

*Mark Minervini's Signature Setup*

### What is VCP?
A series of price contractions where each successive contraction is SMALLER than the previous one, showing that selling pressure is drying up.

### Why VCP Works
- Each contraction "shakes out" weak holders
- By the final contraction, only strong hands remain
- Breakout from tight final contraction = explosive move
- Low-risk entry: stop is close (small final contraction)

### VCP Requirements
1. Prior uptrend (stock already in Stage 2)
2. At least 2 contractions (C1, C2), ideally 3 (C1, C2, C3)
3. Each contraction smaller: e.g., -25%, -15%, -8%
4. Volume declining during contractions
5. Volume surge on breakout (≥1.5× 50DMA)

---

## VCP Anatomy

### Structure

```
C1 (largest correction): -20% to -35%
C2 (medium correction): -10% to -20%
C3 (tight correction):  -5% to -10%
PIVOT: Top of C3 = breakout point
```

### Volume Pattern
- C1: Volume spike on selloff, then declining
- C2: Lower volume than C1
- C3: Lowest volume of the pattern (dry up)
- BREAKOUT: Volume explodes ≥1.5× 50DMA average

### Stop Placement for VCP
- Place stop BELOW the low of C3 (the tightest contraction)
- This gives the tightest risk with the highest reward
- Typical stop distance: 5-8% from pivot

## Market Timing: Breadth Indicators for India

*Know When to Be Aggressive vs. Defensive*

> Individual stock setups fail 75% of the time when the market is in Stage 3 or 4. Check breadth FIRST, then scan.

## Sell Rules — The Most Neglected Trading Skill

*When and How to Exit Every Position*

> Most traders obsess over entries. Professionals obsess over exits. Your sell rules determine your actual return.

### Defensive Sells — When to Cut Loss

| Rule | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Stop-Loss Hit** | Non-negotiable. Stock hits your pre-set stop = EXIT 100%. Do not ask questions. Do not average. |
| **Pattern Failure** | Price falls back into the base after a breakout. False breakout = exit immediately, before stop. |
| **Volume Reversal** | Breakout on massive volume, then price reverses and closes BELOW the pivot. Institutional reversal. |
| **Earnings Miss** | Company reports EPS below expectations after you're in. Sell before the market digests fully. |
| **3 Strikes Rule** | Stock fails to make progress 3 weeks in a row after entry. Capital is stuck = opportunity cost. |

### Offensive Sells — Locking Profits

| Rule | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **First Target Reached** | Stock hits +20-25% target: sell 50%. Let the other 50% trail. Lock in gains, stay in the game. |
| **Climactic Volume Top** | Massive volume spike at a high after a long run = distribution. Sell into strength. |
| **3 Weeks Tight at Top** | Stock consolidates in a tight range 3+ weeks near highs after +30% move = preparing for reversal. |
| **Market Turns Stage 3** | Broader market showing distribution: reduce all positions to 50% regardless of individual charts. |
| **Trailing Stop Activated** | Set stop below each new 10-day closing low. When hit, exit the remaining position. |

## 10 Most Common Mistakes — And the Fix

*Self-Audit: Which of These Are You Making?*

| # | Mistake | Fix |
|---|---------|-----|
| 1 | **Chasing extended breakouts** | >10% past pivot. Late entry = wide stop = terrible R:R. Fix: If it's extended, it's not your trade. Wait for the next base. |
| 2 | **Skipping the Narrow step** | 40 stocks on watchlist = scattered focus = mediocre execution. Fix: Maximum 5 stocks. If it's not top-5, don't trade it. |
| 3 | **No written stop-loss** | 'I'll just watch it' = guaranteed large loss. Fix: Write stop BEFORE clicking Buy. If you can't write it, don't trade. |
| 4 | **Sizing by emotion** | 'I really like this one' = oversizing. Fix: Edge count determines size. Always. No exceptions. |
| 5 | **Averaging down** | Adding to a loser. Doubles your risk in a stock that's already failing your plan. Fix: Only add to WINNERS. |
| 6 | **Trading in all markets** | Buying breakouts in Stage 4 downtrends. Fix: Check Nifty 500 Stage weekly. Reduce when <Stage 2. |
| 7 | **Ignoring volume on breakout** | Price breakout + low volume = false breakout. Fix: Require volume ≥1.5× 50DMA on breakout day. |
| 8 | **Selling too early** | ₹20 target, stock goes ₹200. Fix: Sell 50% at first target, trail the rest below 10-DMA. |
| 9 | **Moving stop to breakeven too fast** | Giving the stock no room to breathe. Fix: Move to breakeven only after +10-15% gain. |
| 10 | **No post-trade review** | Same mistakes repeated month after month. Fix: Journal every trade. Review every Sunday. Fix one error per week. |

**Honest self-assessment:** Score yourself 1 point for each mistake you've made this month. Score > 5 = your journal needs more attention.
